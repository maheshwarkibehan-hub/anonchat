Privacy & Transparency Manifesto

AnonChat was engineered on a foundational tenet: we cannot leak, sell, or disclose data that we never collect. Below is an exact technical accounting of how your identity and messages are protected.

1. Zero Registration & Identity Shield

No accounts, emails, passwords, phone numbers, or social media logins exist on AnonChat. When you connect, you exist solely as a pseudorandom in-memory socket node. We have zero knowledge of who you are.

2. 100% RAM-Only State (Zero Disk Storage)

There is no database, no SQL, no MongoDB, and no Redis attached to AnonChat. Messages pass ephemerally through RAM during socket transit and are never recorded to server hard drives or file logs. When a room closes, the session data is immediately collected by garbage collection.

3. No IP Logging & Minimal Telemetry

Your IP address is used strictly by the network stack to maintain the live WebSocket connection. We do not store, aggregate, or map IP addresses to conversation transcripts.

4. Zero Third-Party Tracking & No Cookies

AnonChat contains zero advertising SDKs, zero Google Analytics, zero Meta Pixels, and zero marketing trackers. Client storage is used exclusively for local UI preferences (such as audio chime mute state).

5. Ephemeral Bombs & In-Memory Voice Notes

Messages marked with the Ephemeral Bomb dissolve automatically in 5 seconds on both screens. Voice notes (up to 10 seconds) are streamed directly in-memory as WebM/Opus audio and are never saved to persistent storage.
